§ 01 — About

What is Fight Club?

Fight Club is an informal mathematics seminar, born inside the Department of Mathematics at the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens. We meet twice a week to tackle material far beyond what any standard course offers.

The club is run by students, for students. Every member presents, battles through exercises, and is expected to fight — with the mathematics, with their own intuitions, and occasionally with the definitions themselves.

Our material has ranged from the foundations of commutative algebra (Atiyah–Macdonald, from scratch) to the geometry of algebraic curves and the Riemann–Roch theorem. The common thread is rigor without mercy, and curiosity without limits.

"The first rule of Fight Club: you do not skip the details of a proof."

New members are always welcome. No background in algebraic geometry is assumed — only the willingness to work hard and to ask "why?" before "how?"
